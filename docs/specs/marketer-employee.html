chorus · employee design · deep archetype

Marketer
a senior IC who turns intent into reach — under a gate.

Where the Engineer ships code and the Analyst ships a belief, the Marketer ships reach — a campaign, a creative, a message that spends budget and touches users. Her output is visible the instant it lands and impossible to recall. So her one failure mode that matters is sending or spending the wrong thing at scale — off-brand, off-budget, off-channel — and the entire design is built around gates, brand-fidelity, and the tournament, not evidence. She has draft reach to everything and go-live reach to nothing without a gate.

01 — The thesis

Depth is a loop, and the blast radius is reach.

chorus's employee is deliberately thin and stateless — "a replayable identity, not a process." The Marketer keeps that invariant. She gets "deep" not by holding state, but by composing durable things into a campaign:

  • A metric and a brand she owns — activation, pipeline, retention — and the voice it all has to sound like.
  • A brand & evidence base — what's on-message, what converted, what burned — accruing in memory.
  • A creative swarm — strategise, generate variants, red-team the brand, run the experiment, in one beat.
  • An open campaign subtree — the live threads of a launch in flight.

What's genuinely new in chorus to make her work — all role-agnostic, so the whole workforce inherits them:

newVariety + read-back

Generate a handful of on-brand candidates, prune the weak ones offline, let the platform pick the winner at serve time — then read the winning element back into memory. The selection engine is mostly the ad platform's; what's ours is the disciplined read-back loop.

newBrand layer

A persistent voice + ontology + anti-personas, wrapped in a pre/live/post validation sandwich. The substrate that makes "on-brand, claim-true" structural, not hoped-for.

newGated write surface

Publish / send / spend WebPlugins bound to secret-refs, fail-closed, capped. The inbound-research counterpart's mirror image: reach out, under a leash.

The crux — what she's governed against

The Analyst is governed against being confidently wrong; her governance is epistemic — a Critic that red-teams the truth. The Marketer is governed against spending or sending recklessly. An LLM will fluently generate a thousand on-budget, off-brand, never-recalled messages — exactly the thing a real marketer's instincts exist to suppress. So her governance is action-class: spend caps, send gates, and a Brand-Critic that red-teams fidelity-and-claims before anything goes live. That single inversion shapes her DoD, her tools, and her authority. The market agrees — every serious vendor converged on "bounded autonomy, approve-before-go-live," and the cautionary tale (§10) is the one big platform whose gate was opt-out.

She owns a metric and a brand, not a question: "grow activation," "launch the v2 positioning," "keep the funnel fed." The channel — content, paid, lifecycle, brand — is config, not code: swap a blog series for an ad set for an email flow and the loop is identical. In chorus a role is exactly three things:

04 — Two modes — IC and under a manager

Same RolePlugin, two compositions.
As an IC

Handed one metric to move

She owns the whole loop end-to-end: frames the hypothesis, runs the swarm, red-teams the brand, stages the go-live for approval, and reads the result back. The default for an open-ended growth mandate where the strategy is the work.

Under a manager

A leaf in a decomposition

A manager splits a launch into per-channel tasks; each becomes a beat assigned to a Marketer. She runs the leaf — one channel's creative + experiment — and the manager integrates across channels. The role is unchanged; only the decomposition moves.

The reach seam — she is the growth organ, not the treasurer

Mira is the natural owner of a growth metric: she reads the funnel and the market and produces the campaigns that move it. But she cannot commit the company's money or audience — no spend above her cap, no send to the full list, no publish to an owned channel, without a gate. She is an IC, not finance and not the brand owner. Her reach flows one way: a staged go-live a human or a manager approves. The seam is draft-and-stage, gate-out — the mirror of the Analyst's read-in, advise-out.

05 — The campaign loop

Signal → hypothesise → generate → eval → ship → measure → back.

Every node is a chorus object. The employee is stateless across the whole loop — continuity is the metric + the brand base + the task rows, never a long-lived Mira. The market calls this the continuous growth loop and is unanimous that the unit of value is the loop on a cadence, not a one-off asset.

The dark node is the signature: ship is a gate, not an action — go-live above a consequence threshold (§09, §11) cannot happen without approval. Where Vera's signature node is red-team the truth, Mira's is red-team the brand, then pass the gate. A long launch may span more than one beat — each rehydrates the open subtree and the brand base where the last left off.

Built — the dark node is closed for publish
The whole ship edge runs live, end-to-end: cms_draft stages an unpublished Strapi draft (invisible on the public blog) → stage_go_live opens the ApprovalGate and blocks the task → a human approves → Mira re-wakes and calls execute_go_live — fail-closed against the ledger, idempotent per approval — and the post appears on the public blog (localhost:1337/blog). Proven by a keyed 3-step e2e (examples/marketer_go_live_run.py). Email SEND is live on the same seam — an approved execute_go_live(email) sends the staged campaign via Resend (from mira@arceus.sh) to a real inbox, markdown rendered to HTML at the transport (examples/marketer_email_send_run.py). Only ad-spend reach is still unbuilt.

06 — The subagents under her

Two tiers, declared in chorus, spawned by dream.

The mechanism is dream's, and it is no longer hypothetical: dream ships a native subagent system — a model-facing spawn_subagent tool, a depth cap, a per-session spawn cap, and enforced capability-minimization (each subagent's tools must be a subset of the parent's, intersected in the live path). That is exactly the "narrower-wins overlay, bounded, depth-capped, ephemeral swarm" this spec assumed — now a runtime guarantee, not a promise.

What chorus owns is the roster: the exact subagents are declared in src/chorus/swarm/ as dream Subagent records — (name, description, tools-subset, skills, spawned_by) — assembled into a SubagentSet the role threads through RoleManifest → build_harness(subagents=…). Tier 1 — role specialists are declared by Mira's plugin; Tier 2 — shared capability agents live in the shared swarm/ registry, reusable across the workforce. The signature specialist is the Brand-Critic — the adversary that keeps her on-message, the exact twin of the Analyst's Critic.

Her skills (know-how, not verbs)

Experiment & measurement

  • A/B discipline — sample size, MDE, power; don't peek, don't p-hack, hold the test.
  • Attribution vs. incrementality — last-touch lies; holdouts and geo-lift over vanity ROAS.
  • Creative testing — pre-flight scoring, multimodal element tagging, element-level win read-back.
  • Forecasting hygiene — CAC/LTV ranges not points, reference classes, scored track record.

The research's blunt finding: the differentiating skill is statistical, not generative. Any model writes the copy; the moat is the measurement discipline.

Brand · channel · craft

  • Brand voice & claim substantiation built — the voice spec, anti-personas, what claims need proof.
  • GEO / AEO + SEO — answer-first structure, schema, entity density, citation-readiness.
  • Deliverability & sender reputation — warmup, list hygiene, the self-limiting ceiling on send.
  • Channel priors — what format and cadence each surface rewards.

Skills vs. memory

Skills ship with the role — the manual, identical for any Marketer on day 1. Memory accrues — which creatives won, which audiences converted, which channel burned, how calibrated her past CAC guesses turned out (§14). Skills are the training; memory is the scar tissue.

09 — How "done" is decided

The DoD bends to the action class, not the epistemic class.

Mira reuses chorus's three existing verifier kinds — no new evaluator. The DoDGenerator picks the one that fits what the beat produced. For her, HumanApproval is the load-bearing case at go-live, and AgentReview (the Brand-Critic) is mandatory before it.

Command built

reversible · machine-checkable

The draft re-renders deterministically, the experiment plan validates, the variant set is well-formed. In-process, no human. The reversible half of the work.

Fits: content draft · creative-set

AgentReview brand built

the Brand-Critic verifies fidelity

The Brand-Critic checks the message is on-voice, every claim is substantiated, no compliance/legal landmine, no asset misuse. This is where "on-budget but off-brand" gets caught. Mandatory above an importance threshold.

Fits: anything going live

HumanApproval go-live built

load-bearing · the gate

The one that defines her. A live send, a spend over cap, a publish to an owned channel — the irreversible reach — signs off with a human. Common here, because her output touches the world the instant it lands.

Fits: sequence · campaign · any spend

Graded, not blanket — risk-tiered approval

10 — Variety, read-back & the brand layer

Her signature primitives — and the cure for confident, on-budget wrongness.

Where Vera's signature is the grounded-assertion layer, Mira's is a pair: a read-back loop that turns served variety into a learned winner, and a brand layer that keeps every candidate on-message. The brand layer is the net-new plumbing; the read-back is a thin loop on top of machinery chorus already has. Both are role-agnostic — any creative employee inherits them.

newVariety + element read-back

Generate a handful of on-brand candidates → prune the weak ones offline → ship the set → let the platform select at serve time → read the winning element back into memory. Not a single best guess, but not an in-house bracket either.

Why so little is ours: the ad platforms (Meta Andromeda, Google PMax) are the serve-time tournament — they test thousands of combinations Mira never could. So she doesn't out-pick them; she feeds them high-variety sets and owns the read-back of which element won. The offline prune is just the Engineer's existing eval pointed at creatives — it kills obvious losers before spend, nothing more. The genuinely new bit is the read-back → calibration loop, below.

newBrand layer — the validation sandwich

A persistent voice model + brand ontology + anti-personas, the load-bearing component the market encodes as a layer, not a per-prompt instruction. Every generation passes a three-stage sandwich:

  • Pre-gen — block prohibited / off-limits prompts before any draft.
  • Live — monitor voice, claims, compliance during generation.
  • Post-gen — the Brand-Critic verifies before anything enters the gate.

Claim substantiation — the marketer's "confidently wrong"

The Analyst's failure mode is a fluent unsourced certainty. Mira's is a fluent unsubstantiated claim — shipped to thousands. So the brand layer encodes which claims need proof, and the Brand-Critic refuses to let an unsubstantiated claim go live. Confidence is bounded by the weakest substantiated link, the exact mechanic of Vera's evidence grades, pointed at marketing claims.

The calibration seam lattice

Per-campaign read-back ships in this spec — winning element, realised lift vs. predicted. The over-time half — scoring predicted CAC/lift against what actually happened, so Mira gets measurably better-calibrated and accrues creative priors — is a reserved lattice seam, exactly like the Analyst's calibration curve. The mechanism (the experiment, the prediction, the won element) lives in chorus; the learning policy that turns 40 campaigns into a calibrated creative instinct is the sibling loop. Reserved, not absorbed.

The cautionary tale — why the gate must be fail-closed

The research's sharpest finding is a negative one. Meta Advantage+'s spend/targeting controls are opt-out, default-on, and re-enable on campaign duplication — making the gate, in independent reporting, "functionally not a gate." The lesson is exact and it is the one chorus already builds: a gate on send/spend must be opt-in, sticky, and fail-closed — never an opt-out toggle. Mira's __post_init__ that refuses to construct a spend grant without a bounded cap is the structural answer to the market leader's mistake.

Status
Variety — built: the Creative subagent drafts on-brand variants over a grounded seed, Mira selects. Claim substantiation — built twice over: brand_lint (deterministic scan) + the Brand-Critic (judgment). Element read-back — deferred into the lattice/memory build by design.

11 — Authority & governance

Graded by reach, not by reversibility of the draft.

Almost everything Mira does up to the gate is reversible — she reads, she reasons, she drafts. Her governance isn't about permission to think; it's about the bar a piece of reach must clear before it touches the world. The wider the reach, the harder the gate.

  • Autonomous — read any funnel/market source, run any offline-eval, draft any creative, stage any campaign in her worktree. Free, reversible, no gate.
  • Brand-gated — anything going live above the importance threshold cannot ship until the Brand-Critic passes (§09).
  • Human-gated — a live send, a spend over cap, a publish to an owned channel; legal too when the claim is regulated.

This invents no new governance machinery: it reuses ApprovalGate, the two-gate budget model, SpendCap / RateCap, the WebPlugin credential leash, and the env secret-ref boundary chorus already ships. Ad and creative spend are first-class cost_events, so a runaway campaign trips gate 2 like any other overrun.

Status
The gate chain is built and exercised end-to-end for publish: stage_go_live opens the AUTHORIZATION ApprovalGate (and now refuses to re-stage while an approved gate awaits execution); execute_go_live is the fail-closed executor — only an APPROVED gate for THIS task publishes, exactly once per approval, with an approved-undelivered gate outranking any accidental duplicate. Both reaches run through it live: blog publish and email send, each proven by a keyed 3-step human-gated e2e. SpendCap/RateCap and the two-gate budget exist in chorus but are not yet bound to a marketer spend verb (no ad.spend yet).

The hard boundary — she is not finance, and not the brand owner

  • YES read the funnel, the market, the brand kit, the whole company brain
  • YES generate variants, run experiments, draft any asset
  • YES stage a campaign and request its go-live
  • NO spend over her cap, or move budget, without a gate
  • NO send to the full list, or publish to an owned channel, unapproved
  • NO edit the OKR tree, redefine the brand, or commit the company

She reaches the world only through a go-live someone chooses to approve. Draft-and-stage, gate-out — the mirror of the Analyst's read-in, advise-out.

The crux — ask vs. assume

a · Block & wait

needy if overused

Task → blocked, ESCALATION, resume on REPLY. Right only when a wrong reading would mis-target the whole spend.

b · Assume & state

reckless if hidden

Make the sensible reading, state the assumption in the brief, keep drafting. Right for low-stakes forks — but never silent, and never past the gate.

c · Graded lean

a good colleague

(b) by default with the assumption logged; (a) when a wrong reading would burn budget or brand. The same consequence-grading as §09, pointed at framing.

Salience — proactive ≠ chatty

The Activity stream is exhaustive and machine-facing (the audit log); the SCRUM_PACKET digest is curated and human-facing. The split is the noise filter: a marketer with read access to the whole funnel could narrate forever — so she surfaces only results past a lift threshold, genuine brand/spend concerns, and go-live requests. Her personality lives in _brief.py: "lead with the metric, then the bet, then the expected lift; show the variant; name the spend; on-brand, always; no hype." Volume scales with trust — proven Mira goes terse, new Mira shows her work.

14 — Tribal knowledge (memory)

Why she gets sharper, not just busier.

Mira reads her campaign memory scope at the start of every beat and appends to it at the end. Over time it accumulates the org's marketing instinct:

  • Creative ledger — every variant, its predicted lift, and — crucially — what it actually did.
  • Channel / audience ledger — which surface and segment converted, which burned out, at what CAC.
  • Brand-voice corpus — the accumulating record of what's on-message and what got flagged off.
  • Dead campaigns — what looked promising and wasn't, so she doesn't re-run a known loser.
The lattice seam

Raw append-only memory is chorus's default. lattice (the sibling consolidation policy) later compresses "ran 40 campaigns, overestimated cold-audience lift" into a learned prior — a calibrated CAC expectation and a sharpened creative instinct. Mira reads the consolidated prior, not the raw 40 rows. The mechanism stays in chorus; the learning policy is the sibling. This is the same seam reserved in §10 — the won-element and the realised lift are the substrate lattice consolidates over. Brand voice itself is a candidate consolidation: the corpus becomes a tighter voice model over time.

Design decisions locked (redirect any of these)

  • No ungated reach — every publish / send / spend is gated; she drafts and stages, a human or manager approves go-live. She is an IC, not finance.
  • Generates & drafts freely — variants, content, experiments to the worktree; her output lands as a staged campaign, not a live one.
  • Outcome kinds = content · creative-set · sequence · campaign; the campaigner "launch the thing" facet is a campaign.
  • Selection is the platform's, not ours — Mira generates on-brand variety, prunes losers with the Engineer's existing eval, and reads the winning element back; the brand layer is the heavy net-new piece. Calibration-over-time is a reserved lattice seam (described, not built here).
  • The swarm is dream-native; chorus only declares the roster — the exact subagents live in src/chorus/swarm/ (shared Tier-2) and the plugin (Tier-1); the spawn mechanism, depth/spawn caps, and tool-intersection are dream's, wired through one RoleManifest.subagents field.
  • Brand-Critic review is mandatory above an importance threshold, graded — skipped for throwaway drafts.
  • The WebPlugin leash is reused, not reinvented — Mira exercises the already-shipped send/spend grades; the fail-closed posture is the answer to the Meta anti-pattern.
  • Integration vendors are placeholders for categories (analytics / ads / SEO-GEO / CMS / publish / lifecycle / brand).
