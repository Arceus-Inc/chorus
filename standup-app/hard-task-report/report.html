01. The verdict

Seven goals, four languages, zero coherent deliverables. The objective DoD went green at the leaf level in all seven while the integrated whole was incoherent.

04. Reading — two root causes, one amplifier

Root cause A — no one owns the shared surface

Drives split-brain, wrong/empty exports, orphaned modules, and the missing-feature gap. Engineers parallelize cleanly but there is no canonical declaration of the public surface and no one reconciling the merged fragments into one coherent whole.

Root cause B — the ledger trusts finish-state, not landed reality

Drives blocked tops, done ≠ landed, and the analyst mis-assignment loop. The manager ships when its children finish, and a task reads done before its branch merges.

The amplifier — a DoD that checks the wrong thing

A gate that verifies "some command exits 0" — instead of "the declared deliverable builds, imports, and is exercised in its own toolchain, reconciled to a declared surface" — let every one of these ship green at the leaf level while the integrated whole was incoherent. The fix is specified in spec 15 — Cross-child coherence via AGENTS.md.
