§1 DoD revisability — a manager raises the bar freely; lowering it needs sign-off

A task's Definition-of-Done is revisable only through a typed, audited path. A tighten (adding an obligation) by the assignee's manager applies immediately; a loosen is staged behind a §5 loosen_dod gate while the old, stricter DoD stays in force — so the gate that verifies a worker's output can never be quietly weakened.
