# Arceus Brand Voice Specification

## Identity
Arceus is an AI company operating system for technical founders. It translates goals into shipped work through autonomous agents, with human governance.

## Tone
- **Technical**: Write for engineers and technical founders. Assume fluency with software development, CI/CD, and team coordination.
- **Direct**: Say what you mean in the fewest words. No filler, no hedging without cause.
- **Confident but grounded**: State capabilities clearly without inflating them. Every performance claim must be evidence-backed.
- **Honest about tradeoffs**: Acknowledge limitations. "Not yet" is better than silence.

## Anti-Personas (never sound like these)
- **Hype marketer**: "Revolutionary!", "Game-changing!", "10x your productivity!"
- **Vague visionary**: "Reimagine the future of work", "Unlock infinite possibilities"
- **Buzzword stacker**: "AI-powered synergistic next-gen platform"
- **Enterprise salesperson**: "Best-in-class enterprise-grade solution"

## Prohibited Phrases
- revolutionary, game-changing, groundbreaking, disruptive
- 10x, 100x (unless citing a measured benchmark)
- magic, magical, automagically
- unlock, unleash, supercharge
- best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge, bleeding-edge
- synergy, leverage (as verb), paradigm shift

## Claim Policy
- Every performance claim MUST be substantiated with a specific metric, benchmark, or citation.
- Comparative claims ("faster than X") require measurement methodology.
- Use "we believe" or "early results suggest" for unvalidated hypotheses.
- Never promise outcomes the product cannot guarantee today.

## Structure Rules
- Lead with the problem, not the product.
- Show, don't tell: concrete examples over abstract descriptions.
- One idea per paragraph. Short paragraphs (3-5 sentences max).
- Headers should be informative, not clever.
